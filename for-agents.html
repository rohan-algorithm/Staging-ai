One vacant listing, staged

If the home is empty, send the photos you already shot. RoomGenix will stage up to 8 of them, without a watermark, in exchange for a short quote and permission to show one before-and-after.

What you get

  • Up to 8 photos of one vacant listing. That is the Listing pass, normally $19.
  • The files are not watermarked. The 3 previews on a new account are separate, and those are watermarked.
  • Furniture goes into the photo you uploaded. Walls, windows, and floors stay as shot.
  • A photo is usually ready in a minute or two.

What we ask for

  • Permission to show one before-and-after on roomgenix.com.
  • A sentence or two, with your first name and market. For example, “Adam — Atlanta.”

Before you publish

MLS-friendly: walls, windows and floors stay as photographed, and you keep the original to post alongside the labeled staged version. That is not a board approval or a guarantee of compliance; your MLS and state rules decide. See is virtual staging legal? for the disclosure basics.

Roomgenix does not print “Virtually staged” on the file, and this offer is not an approval from a board. Check your photo rules before the listing goes live. The MLS photo rules page is a reminder, not legal advice.
