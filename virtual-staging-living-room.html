Virtual staging for empty living rooms

The living room is usually the first photo in a listing and the room buyers judge fastest. RoomGenixAI adds a sofa, rug, coffee table and a few accents to your photo of the empty room, and is built to keep the walls, windows and floor you shot.

Why stage the living room first

An empty living room photographs as a box of floor. Buyers can't tell whether a sectional fits, where the TV goes or how the room connects to the kitchen or dining area. A staged living room answers those questions in one image, which is why it is often the lead photo and the room most worth staging if you only stage one.

What goes in

A typical living room stage includes a sofa or sectional, a coffee table, a large area rug, one or two accent chairs, a side table with a lamp and a plant or piece of art. The goal is a room that looks lived-in but still shows plenty of floor, so buyers see the space rather than the furniture.

Tips for a better result

  • Shoot from a corner or doorway at about chest height so the whole floor is visible.
  • Keep verticals straight and exposure even. A dark or tilted photo stays dark or tilted after staging.
  • For open-plan rooms, frame the living area clearly so the furniture lands in the right zone.
  • Check doors, windows and fireplaces in the result. If something moved, run it again and don't list that file.
