MLS Compliance & Ethics Guide

Updated September 2026 · NAR Standard 12-10

Put a virtually staged note on the file. NAR Standard of Practice 12-10 asks for a disclosure when a photo was altered. This page is not a certificate from NAR or from any MLS.

What's allowed

  • Adding furniture, rugs, art to vacant rooms
  • Removing tenant clutter, boxes, unmade beds
  • Day-to-dusk exterior conversion
  • Outdoor patio staging

What requires a disclaimer

  • Virtual wall repainting
  • Virtual flooring replacement
  • Repurposing rooms (bedroom as office)

What's prohibited

  • Removing power lines, hydrants, utilities
  • Hiding water damage, mold, cracks
  • Falsifying views or surroundings
  • Distorting room dimensions

What this page is not

It is not an approval letter from CRMLS, Bright MLS, Stellar MLS, FMLS, HAR, or any other board.
