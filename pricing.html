What one credit buys

  • One staged, unwatermarked photo at full resolution.
  • Any style and any job: staging, virtual twilight, decluttering or a virtual remodel. Every credit works the same, so the only difference between packs is the price per photo.
  • Downloading the same staged file again is free. A new render of the photo uses another credit.

What it costs for a typical listing

The $19 Listing Pass covers up to 8 staged photos, which is enough to stage the main rooms of most vacant homes. Physical staging for a vacant home commonly runs from about a thousand dollars into several thousand, and hand-edited virtual staging is often tens of dollars a photo. See virtual vs physical staging cost for the full comparison.

Done-for-you

If you would rather send the photos and get staged files back, the done-for-you service is $9 a photo, minimum 3. You pay after you see them.

Refunds

Email support@roomgenix.com within 30 days of a charge and we refund that payment. We don't calculate partial refunds from unused credits. Details are on the refund page.

Questions

Is there a subscription?

No. You pay per photo or per pack, and nothing renews.

Do credits expire?

No. Credits from any pack stay on the account as long as it is open.

Do I need a card to try it?

No. A new account includes 3 watermarked previews without a card.

Do bigger packs include more styles?

No. Every pack can use every style and job. Bigger packs only lower the price per photo.
