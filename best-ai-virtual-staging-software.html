Subscription or pay per photo?

This is the biggest difference between tools. A subscription is cheapest per photo if you use most of your monthly allowance every month, which suits photographers and busy teams. If you stage a few listings a month, or your volume goes up and down, you can end up paying for photos you don't use. Pay-per-photo tools cost more per image at high volume but nothing in a quiet month. RoomGenixAI is pay-per-photo, and its credits don't expire.

What to test before you choose

  • Does the room stay the same? Run a before and after and compare windows, doors, floors and ceiling lines. Some AI tools redraw the room, which can misrepresent the property.
  • Is the furniture to scale? Oversized or tiny furniture makes a room look misleadingly large or small.
  • How many tries does a usable photo take? A cheap per-photo price matters less than the number of reruns you need.
  • What does it cost for your real month? Multiply your typical number of staged photos by each tool's actual price, including any unused subscription allowance.
  • Can you disclose easily? Most tools, RoomGenixAI included, don't stamp a label on the file. Plan how you'll label photos for your MLS.

When a human editor is the better choice

Hand-edited services like BoxBrownie cost more and take longer, but a designer can handle unusual rooms, specific furniture requests and tricky lighting that AI tools still get wrong. For a high-end listing where one hero photo matters a lot, that can be worth it.

Questions

What is the cheapest AI virtual staging software?

Per photo, high-volume subscriptions are usually cheapest if you use the full allowance. For a few listings a month, pay-per-photo tools such as RoomGenixAI often cost less overall.

Is AI virtual staging good enough for the MLS?

It can be, if the room itself is unchanged, the furniture is to scale and the photo is disclosed according to your board's rules.
