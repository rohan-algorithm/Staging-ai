About RoomGenixAI

RoomGenixAI is an AI virtual staging tool for real estate listing photos. It adds furniture to a photo of an empty room and is built to keep the walls, windows and floor you photographed.

Who builds it

I'm Rohan, and I build RoomGenixAI on my own. There is no sales team. When you email support@roomgenix.com, the reply comes from me, and so do the staged photos in the done-for-you service.

Why it exists

Many AI image tools redraw the whole room when you ask for furniture. Windows move, floors change and the photo stops matching the house. That is a problem for a listing, because buyers walk in expecting the room in the picture. RoomGenixAI puts furniture into the photo you already shot instead of generating a new room. How it keeps the room layout explains the approach and where it can still go wrong.

What it does

  • Stages empty living rooms, bedrooms, dining rooms and home offices in styles such as modern, Scandinavian and farmhouse.
  • Turns daytime exteriors into twilight shots and removes clutter from occupied rooms.
  • Usually returns a photo in a minute or two. Check every result before you list it.
